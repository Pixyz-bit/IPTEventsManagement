1. Executive Summary & Lifecycle Architecture

The Enterprise University Event Management and Dynamic Ticketing System governs the end-to-end lifecycle of university-wide events, co-curricular assemblies, and departmental symposia[cite: 1]. It unifies administrative event governance, pre-verified student eligibility enforcement, real-time gate admission via encrypted optical QR tokens, and tamper-resistant registrar accreditation clearance[cite: 1].

Pre-Provisioned Registrar Store[cite: 1]

Enforces student identity verification via immutable Student ID, University Branch, Department, and Degree Course records[cite: 1].

Live Gate Terminal (< 2.0s Validation)[cite: 1]

Real-time optical scanner supporting 4 instant verification states: Access Granted, Duplicate Alert, Revoked, and Invalid Record[cite: 1].

Targeted Catalog Discovery[cite: 1]

Dynamic event display filtered across 4 cohort restriction vectors: Campus Branch, Department, Degree Program, and Year Level[cite: 1].

The Dynamic Cancellation Bounce[cite: 1]

Automatically reopens fully-booked events upon student self-cancellation strictly within the open registration window[cite: 1].

3. Student Profile Pre-Provisioning & Account Security

Anti-Fraud Identity Mandate: Open student self-registration is strictly prohibited[cite: 1]. All student user accounts are batch-ingested by institutional administrators directly from verified registrar master records to eliminate fictitious identities and duplicate seat reservations[cite: 1].

  • Pre-Provisioned Master Fields: Student Identification Number (Primary Key), Legal Last Name, Legal First Name, Gender, University Branch, College/Department, Degree Program/Course, Institutional Email (@university.edu), and Temporary Placeholder Password[cite: 1].
  • Forced Credential Update: Upon initial login, students are immediately routed to a mandatory password change screen[cite: 1]. Access to event browsing, registration, and dashboard features is locked until new credentials are confirmed[cite: 1].
  • Profile Immutability: All registrar-provisioned placement fields remain read-only on the student dashboard[cite: 1]. Discrepancies must be escalated to the university registrar to prevent unauthorized cohort shifts[cite: 1].

5. Student Registration & Dynamic Cancellation Engine

5.1 Booking Workflow & 1-Student/1-Ticket Invariant

  1. Targeted Catalog Discovery: Eligible students view tailored event cards displaying schedules, venue locations, host badges, registration countdown timers, and live seating availability[cite: 1].
  2. Verification & Submission: Selecting an event opens a confirmation dialogue displaying read-only identity fields (Student ID, Full Name, Gender, Branch, Department, and Course)[cite: 1]. The student verifies their Year Level and enters their assigned Section[cite: 1].
  3. One-Ticket Rule: The database enforces an absolute constraint of one active ticket per student per event at the transactional boundary[cite: 1]. Duplicate booking attempts are blocked[cite: 1].
  4. Issuance & QR Delivery: Upon confirmation, the live seat count increments, the event appears on the student dashboard, and an encrypted QR ticket is dispatched to their verified institutional email[cite: 1].

5.2 Window-Tethered Cancellation & "The Cancellation Bounce"

  • The Cancellation Bounce: When an event reaches maximum capacity (e.g., 200/200), its status switches to FULLY_BOOKED and registration locks[cite: 1]. If a student cancels while the registration window remains open, the count drops to 199/200, and status immediately flips back to OPEN, unlocking public registration without administrator intervention[cite: 1].
  • Registration Window Cutoff: Students can cancel their registration directly from their personal dashboard strictly while the registration window remains active (prior to Registration End Date/Time)[cite: 1].
  • Permanent Operational Roster Freeze: As soon as the registration window closes, the self-cancellation button is permanently hidden and disabled on all student dashboards[cite: 1]. Final rosters freeze to allow administrators to plan venue resources and identify true no-shows[cite: 1].
  • Scan Lockout: Once a student's QR ticket is scanned at the venue entrance, its status updates to CHECKED_IN, permanently revoking self-cancellation privileges regardless of remaining window time[cite: 1].

System operations, registration buttons, and cancellation controls are governed by a deterministic priority hierarchy[cite: 1]. Higher-tier administrative overrides supersede lower-tier automatic state conditions[cite: 1]:

MANUALLY LOCKED (Tier 2)[cite: 1] No[cite: 1] No[cite: 1] No[cite: 1] Admin triggers "Close Registration Early"[cite: 1]. Manual kill switch overrides capacity rules and prevents reopening[cite: 1].
REGISTRATION CLOSED (Tier 3)[cite: 1] No[cite: 1] No[cite: 1] No[cite: 1] Current Time >= Registration End Timestamp[cite: 1]. Roster locks; self-service cancellation permanently disabled[cite: 1].
FULLY BOOKED (Tier 4)[cite: 1] No[cite: 1] Yes[cite: 1] Yes[cite: 1] Confirmed registrations reach 100% capacity during active window[cite: 1]. A cancellation dynamically reopens the event to OPEN[cite: 1].
OPEN (Tier 5)[cite: 1] Yes[cite: 1] Yes[cite: 1] N/A[cite: 1] Active registration window, available capacity, and no active administrative overrides[cite: 1].

7. Admin Phase 1: Planning & Pre-Registration Monitoring

Following event configuration, administrators monitor capacity and manage rosters through a real-time command dashboard[cite: 1]:

7.1 Event Overview & Live Controls

  • Dynamic Header & Status Badge: Displays real-time operational status (OPEN, FULLY_BOOKED, REGISTRATION_CLOSED, MANUALLY_LOCKED, or CANCELLED)[cite: 1].
  • Core Logistics Card: Shows schedule, physical venue, hall capacity, and an active registration deadline countdown timer[cite: 1].
  • Capacity Progress Meter: Live visual gauge tracking reserved slots against venue limits (e.g., 210 / 250 Seats Taken (84%))[cite: 1]. Public registration locks automatically upon reaching 100% capacity[cite: 1].
  • Administrative Action Controls:
    • Edit Event: Updates schedule, venue, or host details, automatically sending notification emails to all registered students[cite: 1].
    • Close Registration Early: Engages the MANUALLY_LOCKED state override to freeze registrations and prevent reopening[cite: 1].
    • Cancel Event: Prompts for a mandatory cancellation reason, voids all issued tickets, and dispatches batch cancellation emails[cite: 1].
    • Resend All Ticket Reminders: Re-transmits QR code tickets to all confirmed attendees[cite: 1].

7.2 Pre-Registration Roster Table

  • Attendee Data Columns: Student Number, Full Legal Name, University Branch, College/Department, Degree Program/Course, Year Level, Section, Registration Timestamp, and Ticket Delivery Status (Sent or Failed)[cite: 1].
  • Search & Filter Tools: Search by Name or Student ID; filter records by Campus Branch, Department, Year Level, or Section[cite: 1].
  • Roster Operations: Single-student ticket resend utility for attendees reporting lost or filtered confirmation emails[cite: 1].

8.2 Optical QR Scanner & 4-State Verification Terminal

[GREEN] ACCESS GRANTED – VALID TICKET[cite: 1]

First-time valid scan[cite: 1]. Displays Full Name, Student Number, Department, Course, Year & Section[cite: 1]. Emits success chime, records entry timestamp, grants admission[cite: 1].

[RED] DUPLICATE ADMISSION ALERT[cite: 1]

Ticket already checked in[cite: 1]. Displays original check-in timestamp and gate terminal identifier to prevent pass-back fraud; emits warning buzzer[cite: 1].

[AMBER] REVOKED – REGISTRATION CANCELLED[cite: 1]

Ticket cancelled by student during the open window[cite: 1]. Displays student identity and cancellation timestamp; denies entrance[cite: 1].

[DARK-GRAY] INVALID TICKET – RECORD NOT FOUND[cite: 1]

Unrecognized or corrupted cryptographic QR token not associated with this event[cite: 1]. Denies entry; refers student to admin desk[cite: 1].

8.3 Operational Fallbacks & Gate Exceptions

  • Manual Lookup & Override: Search input by Student Number or Last Name to admit attendees with dead phone batteries or damaged screens[cite: 1]. Manual admissions require a mandatory reason flag (e.g., Dead Battery, Unsent Email) for audit compliance[cite: 1].
  • Dual-Filter Attendance Roster: Live split-table interface separating registered students into Present and Pending Arrival tabs with real-time search[cite: 1].
  • Supervisor Scan Rollback: Admin capability to reverse accidental scans, returning the record to Pending Arrival and adjusting headcount metrics, while keeping student self-service cancellation locked[cite: 1].
