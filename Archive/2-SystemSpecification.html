University Event Management & Gate Clearance System

Operational Requirements Specification (SRS) • Two-Role Architecture

Target Environment: Higher Education Operations | System Roles: Institutional Administrator & Student Attendee

1. Operational Roles & Access Hierarchy

Operational RoleCore Capabilities & Administrative ScopeTouchpoints & System Tools
Institutional Administrator (Admin) Provisions student identities, configures logistics and audience matrices, monitors registration rosters, operates live gate check-in scanners, executes manual lookups/rollbacks, and generates official attendance PDF/Excel clearance reports. Admin Portal, Event Wizard, Gate Terminal Console, Headcount Deck, Post-Mortem Analytics, Master Ledger Hub.
Student Attendee (User) Browses tailored event listings, verifies year/section info, books tickets (enforcing one active booking per event), receives unique QR pass via email, and manages self-service cancellations within the active registration window. Student Portal, Profile Password Manager, Personal Dashboard, Automated QR Pass Email.

2. Student Identity Provisioning & Authentication

Public open registration is permanently disabled. Student master records are provisioned directly by admins from official registrar databases with immutable fields: Student Number, Last Name, First Name, Gender, University Branch, Department, Course, and Email. Accounts receive a placeholder password;

3. Event Parameters & Target Audience Matrix

Admins configure mandatory logistics: Event Name, Description, Date & Time, Venue Location, Hosting Organization & Sponsors, Total Seating Capacity, and Registration Window (Start/End). Events are restricted via a 4-tier filtering matrix:

  • University Branch: Targets specific satellite campuses or primary campus units.
  • Department / College: Filters visibility to enrolled colleges (e.g., Engineering, Business).
  • Degree Program / Course: Limits specialized seating strictly to approved degree majors.
  • Academic Year Level: Enforces cohort restrictions (e.g., 1st Year Orientation, 4th Year Practicum).

5. Registration Flow & The Cancellation Bounce

Eligible students review pre-filled profile data, fill in mandatory Year Level and Section fields, and submit their reservation. The system records the booking under a strict rule of one active registration per student per event and transmits a unique cryptographic QR ticket via email.

Cancellation Rules: Students may cancel directly from their personal dashboard strictly while the registration window remains active. Cancelling decrements active bookings and triggers The Cancellation Bounce: if the event was FULLY_BOOKED, it automatically re-opens to OPEN. Once the registration window ends or a QR ticket is scanned at the venue, self-cancellation is permanently locked.

6. Live Execution Phase & Admin Gatekeeper Verification

Admins operate the gatekeeper terminal using camera or barcode gun input (< 2.0s throughput). The system evaluates tickets across four high-visibility verification displays:

Manual Lookup & Rollback: If a student has a dead phone or technical issue, admins search by Student Number or Last Name to perform a manual check-in with an audit flag. An accidental check-in can be reverted via a supervisor rollback button, keeping student self-service cancellation locked.
