1. Executive Summary & Lifecycle Architecture

The Enterprise University Event Management and Dynamic Ticketing System governs the end-to-end lifecycle of university-wide events, co-curricular assemblies, and departmental symposia. It unifies administrative event governance, pre-verified student eligibility enforcement, real-time gate admission via encrypted optical QR tokens, and tamper-resistant registrar accreditation clearance.

2. Institutional Role Hierarchy & Governance Matrix

The system enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) across four operational tiers:

Institutional Role Permitted Operational Scope Access Constraints & Authentication
Institutional Administrator Full event creation, core logistics management, manual early lock execution, event cancellation, student pre-provisioning, manual ticket resends, and accreditation sign-off. MFA-protected Administrative Portal; master institution credentials.
Student Attendee Discovery of targeted event feeds, one-click reservation confirmation, QR ticket dashboard access, and self-service cancellation strictly during the open registration window. Verified university credentials with mandatory first-login password update.
Gatekeeper / Door Marshal Optical QR check-in scanning (< 2.0s ingestion), manual roster search for dead-battery exceptions, and supervisor scan rollbacks. Restricted Live Gate Terminal; access limited to event hours.
Clearance & Audit Officer Review of demographic and velocity dashboards, master attendance verification, and generation of official excuse documents. Read-only departmental audit deck with registrar-level signing clearance.

3. Student Profile Pre-Provisioning & Account Security

Anti-Fraud Identity Mandate: Open student registration is strictly prohibited. All user accounts are batch-ingested by institutional administrators directly from verified registrar master records to eliminate duplicate accounts and fraudulent seat reservations.
  • Pre-Provisioned Master Fields: Student Identification Number (Primary Key), Legal Last Name, Legal First Name, Gender, University Branch, College/Department, Degree Program/Course, Institutional Email (@university.edu), and Temporary Placeholder Password.
  • Forced Credential Update: Upon initial login, students are redirected to a mandatory password change screen. Access to event browsing, registration, and dashboard features is completely restricted until the student confirms their new private credentials.
  • Profile Immutability: All registrar-provisioned fields remain read-only on the student dashboard. Inconsistencies must be resolved through registrar channels to prevent unauthorized profile alterations.

5. Student Registration & Dynamic Cancellation Engine

The registration and cancellation workflow maintains database consistency and enforces fairness across high-demand university events:

5.1 Booking Workflow & 1-Student / 1-Ticket Invariant

  1. Targeted Catalog Discovery: Eligible students view tailored event cards showing logistics, sponsors, countdown timers, and live seating counts.
  2. Verification & Submission: Selecting an event opens a confirmation view displaying the student's read-only details (Student ID, Full Name, Gender, Branch, Department, and Course). The student verifies their current Year Level and enters their assigned Section.
  3. One-Ticket Rule: The database enforces an absolute constraint of one active ticket per student per event. Duplicate booking attempts are blocked at the transactional boundary.
  4. Issuance & QR Delivery: Upon confirmation, the live seat count increments, the event appears on the student's dashboard, and an encrypted QR ticket is dispatched to their institutional email.

5.2 Window-Tethered Cancellation & "The Cancellation Bounce"

  • The Cancellation Bounce: When an event reaches maximum capacity (e.g., 200/200), its status switches to FULLY_BOOKED and registration locks. If a student cancels while the registration window remains open, the count drops to 199/200, and the status immediately flips back to OPEN. The public registration button is unlocked instantly without requiring manual administrative intervention.
  • Registration Window Cutoff: Students can cancel their registration directly from their dashboard strictly while the registration window is active (prior to Registration End Date/Time).
  • Permanent Operational Roster Freeze: As soon as the registration window closes, the self-cancellation button is permanently hidden and disabled on all student dashboards. Final rosters freeze to allow administrators to plan venue resources and identify true no-shows.
  • Scan Lockout: Once a student's QR code is scanned at the venue entrance, their ticket status updates to CHECKED_IN, permanently revoking self-cancellation capabilities regardless of any remaining window time.

6. Deterministic 5-Tier Event State Machine

System operations, booking buttons, and cancellation controls are governed by a 5-tier priority hierarchy. Higher-tier administrative overrides supersede lower-tier state conditions:

System State Registration? Self-Cancel? Dynamic Bounce? Trigger Conditions & Precedence Rules
CANCELLED (Tier 1) No No No Admin cancels event; mandatory reason logged; tickets invalidated; batch notification emails sent.
MANUALLY_LOCKED (Tier 2) No No No Admin triggers "Close Registration Early". Manual kill switch overrides capacity rules and prevents reopening.
REGISTRATION_CLOSED (Tier 3) No No No Current Time >= Registration End Timestamp. Roster locks; self-service cancellation permanently disabled.
FULLY_BOOKED (Tier 4) No Yes Yes Confirmed registrations reach 100% capacity during active window. A cancellation dynamically reopens the event to OPEN.
OPEN (Tier 5) Yes Yes N/A Active registration window, available capacity, and no active administrative overrides.

7. Admin Phase 1: Planning & Pre-Registration Monitoring

Following event creation, administrators track registrations and enforce logistics through a real-time command dashboard:

7.1 Event Overview & Live Controls

  • Dynamic Header & Status Badge: Displays the current state (OPEN, FULLY_BOOKED, REGISTRATION_CLOSED, MANUALLY_LOCKED, or CANCELLED).
  • Core Logistics Card: Shows event schedule, venue location, room capacity, and an active countdown timer tracking the registration deadline.
  • Capacity Progress Meter: Live visual gauge tracking reserved seats against total capacity (e.g., 210 / 250 Seats Taken (84%)). Public registration locks automatically at 100%.
  • Administrative Action Controls:
    • Edit Event: Update logistics. Modifying the date, time, or venue automatically dispatches updated email notifications to all registered students.
    • Close Registration Early: Triggers the MANUALLY_LOCKED kill switch to prevent further registrations and freeze cancellations (e.g., in response to venue maintenance).
    • Cancel Event: Prompts for a mandatory cancellation reason, marks all tickets void, and dispatches batch cancellation emails.
    • Resend All Ticket Reminders: Re-sends QR ticket emails to all confirmed attendees.

7.2 Pre-Registration Roster Table

A searchable, filterable data table displaying all registered attendees:

  • Data Columns: Student Number, Full Name, University Branch, College/Department, Degree Program/Course, Year Level, Section, Registration Timestamp, and Ticket Delivery Status (Sent or Failed).
  • Search & Filter Tools: Search by Name or Student ID; filter by Campus Branch, Department, Year Level, or Section.
  • Roster Operations: Single-student ticket resend tool for attendees reporting lost or filtered emails.

8.2 Optical QR Scanner & 4-State Verification Terminal

[ GREEN ] ACCESS GRANTED — VALID TICKET First-time scan confirmation.
Displays: Full Name, Student Number, Department, Course, Year & Section.
Action: Commits timestamp, emits an audio chime, grants admission.
[ RED ] DUPLICATE ADMISSION ALERT High-visibility security alert: Ticket already scanned.
Displays: Original check-in timestamp and gate terminal identifier.
Action: Emits a warning buzzer; blocks ticket pass-back fraud.
[ AMBER ] REVOKED — REGISTRATION CANCELLED Ticket cancelled by student during the open window.
Displays: Student identity and cancellation timestamp.
Action: Denies admission; prevents use of cancelled email tickets.
[ DARK-GRAY ] INVALID TICKET — RECORD NOT FOUND Unrecognized or corrupted cryptographic QR token.
Displays: Unregistered token signature warning.
Action: Denies entry; refers student to the help desk.

8.3 Operational Fallbacks & Gate Exceptions

  • Manual Lookup & Override: Search by Student Number or Last Name to admit attendees with dead phone batteries or damaged screens. Manual entries require an operational reason flag (e.g., Dead Battery, Unsent Email) for audit logging.
  • Dual-Filter Attendance Roster: A live, split-table view separating attendees into Present and Pending Arrival tabs with real-time search.
  • Supervisor Scan Rollback: Authorized gate supervisors can undo accidental scans, returning the ticket to Pending Arrival and adjusting turnout gauges, while keeping student self-cancellation locked.

9. Admin Phase 3: Post-Event Analytics & Clearance Hub

Following event completion, the system transitions to compliance tracking, accreditation reporting, and institutional archiving:

9.1 Performance Analytics & Velocity Curves

  • Final Turnout Metrics: Final summary comparing total room capacity, pre-registrations, verified attendees present, final turnout percentage, and net no-shows.
  • Audience Purity Percentage: Percentage of attendees who strictly matched the target audience matrix versus any manual exceptions granted.
  • Demographic Breakdown Visualizations:
    • Attendance distribution across Colleges and Academic Departments.
    • Turnout segmented by Year Level and Section.
    • Comparative attendance rates across University Branches.
  • Arrival Velocity Curve: A time-series chart tracking scan volume in 10- or 15-minute intervals. Identifies peak ingress windows and entrance line bottlenecks to inform future gate staffing.

9.2 Master Audit & Clearance Export Hub

The system generates two official clearance deliverables for academic administration:

1. Master Raw Dataset (.xlsx)

An unaggregated operational dataset designed for registrar integration, including Student Identification Number, Full Legal Name, University Branch, College/Department, Degree Program, Year Level, Section, Registration Timestamp, Gate Entry Timestamp, Gate Terminal Identifier, and Verification Mode (Optical Scan vs. Manual Override with audit notes).

10. Conceptual Data Model & Entity Relationships

The conceptual entities, functional attributes, and relational constraints governing system operations:

Conceptual Entity Key Functional Attributes Relational Invariants & Integrity Constraints
STUDENTS
(Master Ingestion)		 Student_ID (PK), First_Name, Last_Name, Gender, Branch_Name, Department_Name, Course_Name, University_Email, Password_Hash, Password_Change_Required. Pre-provisioned by administrators. Student_ID and University_Email are globally unique. Profile placement fields remain immutable to the student.
EVENTS
(Event Configuration)		 Event_ID (PK), Title, Description, Venue_Location, Max_Capacity, Current_Registrations, Event_Start, Event_End, Reg_Window_Start, Reg_Window_End, Target_Filters, Status, Manual_Lock_Flag. Status is governed by the 5-tier state machine. Dynamic reopening occurs only if Manual_Lock_Flag == FALSE and current time is within the registration window.
TICKETS / ROSTER
(Junction Entity)		 Ticket_ID (PK), Event_ID (FK), Student_ID (FK), Year_Confirmed, Section_Confirmed, Ticket_Token, Status (ACTIVE, CHECKED_IN, REVOKED), Reg_Timestamp, CheckIn_Timestamp, Gate_ID, Override_Reason. Enforces a strict compound unique constraint on (Event_ID, Student_ID) for active tickets. Self-cancellation is permitted only during an active registration window.

11. Operational Edge Case Resolution Matrix

The Cancellation Bounce FULLY_BOOKED during active registration window A student cancellation decrements the count from $N$ to $N-1$. The system automatically flips the status back to OPEN and enables public registration.

Schedule or Venue Relocation Administrative update to an active event Saving modified event logistics triggers automated email updates to all confirmed ticket holders.
Event Cancellation CANCELLED triggered by admin Prompts for a mandatory reason, sets all tickets to void, and sends batch cancellation notification emails to all registrants.
